Tarika (research-adharit): (1) Score = trend (SMA20/50), MACD, RSI, Bollinger, momentum, volume, NEPSE index sanga 60-din tulana, risk (volatility/drawdown), valuation ra news. (2) Prob gain ra Exit = tyo stock ko pachhillo ~1 barsa ma yehi trend avastha bata suru bhayeko 15/30 din ko asli return ko distribution (median, P10, P90), abhiko volatility anusar milaieko. (3) Entry = pullback/support najik; Exit = median target (resistance agadi); Stop = support talo ra 2xATR. (4) Backtest: yehi niyam le pachhillo barsa ma kati pataka naafa dieko (confidence). (5) Kharcha: broker ~0.4% x2, SEBON 0.015% x2, DP Rs 25, CGT 7.5% naafa ma (aafno broker sanga verify garnus). Sima: NEPSE ma ek din ±10% circuit hunchha, stop-loss ma bikna garo huna sakchha. Backtest ko win rate bhavishya ko garanti haina. Yo financial salaha haina.
